World builder

Draw a road network. Roads, markings, buildings and trees are generated from the graph.

  • Click empty space to add a node, chained from the selected one
  • Click a road to insert a junction
  • Drag a node to move it
  • Right-click to deselect, or delete a node / road
  • Wheel zoom · Space + drag or middle-drag pan

Neuro-evolution

Each car is driven by its own neural network. The fittest survive, breed and mutate.

Test drive

  • Any driving key takes back control from autopilot
